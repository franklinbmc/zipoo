Privacy Policy

Zipoo values your privacy. We store and manage your business records strictly to operate your POS, inventory, accounting, and sales operations. We never sell your personal or financial data to advertisers.

Last updated: October 2026 • United Republic of Tanzania

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zipoo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our business management applications, websites, APIs, and progressive web apps (collectively, the "Services").

By creating an account or using Zipoo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy and the relevant data protection regulations of Tanzania.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, as well as data generated automatically during your business operations:

  • Account & Profile Details: Full name, phone number, email address, password hash, region, district, and business name during registration.
  • Business & Operational Data: Store names, warehouse locations, product inventory, retail prices, cost prices, categories, barcodes, and supplier details.
  • Sales & Financial Records: Customer details, invoice lines, payment methods (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money), VAT settings, expense vouchers, shift reports, and rental records.
  • Communications & Messaging: Recipient phone numbers, sender ID preferences, and transaction receipts dispatched via our SMS gateway.
  • Device & Offline Storage Data: Local storage and IndexedDB caches on your phone, tablet, or desktop to enable complete offline access and automatic sync.

3. How We Use Information

We use the data collected for the following operational purposes:

  • To calculate, record, and display your business point-of-sale sales, inventory levels, accounts, and financial dashboards.
  • To enable offline operation, allowing you to record sales, stock adjustments, and expenses without an internet connection and sync them automatically once reconnected.
  • To deliver transactional SMS notifications, OTP authentication codes, and digital receipt summaries to your customers and staff.
  • To enforce role-based access control (RBAC), ensuring team members only access records approved by business owners.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent unauthorized account access, and preserve system audit trails.

4. Data Protection & Security

We employ modern security standards to safeguard your records. All communication between your device and our servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

User passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard Bcrypt / Argon2 algorithms and are never stored in plaintext. Offline records stored in your browser or app cache are tied to your device domain and can be cleared whenever you log out.

5. Sharing & Service Providers

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to advertisers. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to fulfill core services:

  • Telecom & SMS Aggregators (MegaSMS): Transmitting verification OTPs, payment receipts, and customer alerts via SMS.
  • Mail Transfer Agents (SMTP): Sending system password resets and notification emails.
  • Payment Processors: Processing billing and subscription packages if authorized by you.

6. Data Ownership & User Rights

You retain full ownership of your business data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Access and review your stored profile and business details in the Settings section.
  • Export your sales reports, customer statements, and stock summaries to PDF or spreadsheets.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of your account by contacting our administrative support.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling in Zipoo, please contact us through the Zipoo Support menu or reach your account administrator.
