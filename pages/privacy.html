Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Zipoo Business Platform
Summary: Zipoo values your privacy. We store and manage your business records solely to operate your POS, inventory, accounting, and sales operations. We never sell your personal or financial data to advertisers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zipoo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our business management applications, websites, APIs, and progressive web apps (collectively, the "Services").

By creating an account or using Zipoo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy and the relevant data protection regulations of Tanzania.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, as well as data generated automatically during your operations:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, phone number, email address, password hash, region, district, and business name during registration.
  • Business & Operational Data: Store names, warehouse locations, product inventory, retail prices, cost prices, categories, barcodes, and supplier details.
  • Sales & Financial Records: Customer details, invoice lines, payment methods (Cash, Bank, Mobile Money), VAT settings, expense vouchers, shift reports, and rental records.
  • Communications & Messaging: SMS delivery recipient numbers, sender ID preferences, and transaction receipts dispatched via our SMS gateway.
  • Device & Offline Storage Data: Local storage and IndexedDB caches on your phone, tablet, or desktop to enable complete offline access and background synchronization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected for the following operational purposes:

  • To operate, calculate, and present your business POS, inventory levels, accounts, and financial dashboards.
  • To enable offline operation, allowing you to record sales, stock adjustments, and expenses without an internet connection and sync them automatically once reconnected.
  • To deliver transactional SMS notifications, OTP authentication codes, and digital receipt summaries to your customers and team.
  • To provide role-based access control (RBAC), ensuring staff members only access records approved by business owners or administrators.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent unauthorized account access, and perform audit logging.

4. Data Protection & Security

We employ modern security standards to safeguard your data. All communication between your device and our servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard Bcrypt / Argon2 algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.

Offline records stored in your browser or app cache are tied to your device domain and can be cleared whenever you log out.

5. Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to fulfill our core services:

  • Telecom & SMS Aggregators (MegaSMS): Transmitting verification OTPs, payment receipts, and customer alerts via SMS.
  • Mail Transfer Agents (SMTP): Sending system password resets and notification emails.
  • Payment Processors: Processing billing and subscription payments if and when authorized by you.

6. Data Ownership & User Rights

You retain full ownership of your business data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Access and review your stored profile and business details in the Settings section.
  • Export your sales reports, customer statements, and stock summaries to PDF or spreadsheets.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information or deletion of your account by contacting our administrative support.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling in Zipoo, please contact us through the Zipoo Support menu or your account administrator.
